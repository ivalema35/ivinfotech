What Does a Website Development Company in India Do?

A website development company in India plans, designs, builds and maintains websites and web applications for businesses. The work usually covers UI/UX design, front-end and back-end development, CMS or e-commerce setup, speed and SEO optimisation, testing, hosting support and post-launch maintenance. A good partner also explains costs and timelines clearly before the project starts.

Website Development Cost in India

The cost of building a website in India depends on the number of pages, design complexity, features, integrations and whether you need a CMS, e-commerce or a custom web application. A simple business website costs much less than a custom portal with dashboards and payment integrations. The table shows what affects the price in each case and how long it usually takes.

Project typeWhat affects the priceTypical timeline
Basic business website (5-8 pages) Number of pages, custom design, content, forms 2-4 weeks
Corporate / CMS website Page count, CMS setup, integrations, content migration 4-6 weeks
E-commerce website Number of products, payment gateways, shipping, inventory 6-10 weeks
Custom web application / portal Features, user roles, integrations, reports 10-16 weeks or more

Every project is different, so we give an itemised quote after understanding your requirements. Share what you want to build and we will tell you what it will cost and how long it will take.
